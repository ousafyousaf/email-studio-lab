## 1. Campaign overview

**Campaign name:** *Doors Open at The Steep Club*

**Summary:** A warm, unhurried four-week email series that invites our waitlist to become founding members of The Steep Club, with a different path for beginners, gift buyers and daily tea drinkers.

**Main goal:** 300 waitlist members start a Steep Club subscription between 7 December 2026 and 3 January 2027.
That means about **15% of the roughly 2,000 people on the waitlist**.

**Secondary goals:**
- Find out which of the three groups converts best, so we know who to focus on next.
- Keep unsubscribes low (under 0.5% per send), because the people who don't join this month are still our future members.
- Collect real words from new members (replies, first-cup feedback) that we can use in later campaigns.

**A note on the target:** 15% is ambitious for email alone. It's realistic because these people asked to hear from us, but only if the list is fresh and the offer is clear. Section 9 covers what we do if we fall behind.

## 2. Target audience

All 2,000 people chose to join our waitlist, so they already know us and are somewhere between *considering* and *ready to decide*. Our job is to make joining feel easy and right, not to persuade strangers.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What holds them back | Buying stage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tea-curious beginners** | Like the idea of loose-leaf tea but don't know where to start. | Guidance, a gentle start, no "tea snob" feel. | Worried they'll brew it wrong, or won't like it. Unsure a subscription is worth it. | Consideration |
| **Gift buyers** | Shopping for someone else, mostly in December. | An easy, thoughtful gift that feels personal. | Will it arrive in time? Can I choose the length? What does the person actually get? | Decision (time-sensitive, because of the holidays) |
| **Daily tea drinkers** | Already brew every day and care about the leaf. | Variety, quality and never running out. | Already have favourite teas. "Is this better than what I buy now?" | Decision |

**Short profiles:**
- *Beginner:* "Someone who wants a calmer daily ritual but feels lost in a tea aisle. They'll join if we make the first step feel simple and friendly."
- *Gift buyer:* "Someone hunting for a December gift that isn't another candle. They'll join if gifting is easy and the timing is clear."
- *Daily drinker:* "Someone with a kettle that's always on. They'll join if the club brings them leaves they wouldn't find on their own."

**Where they spend time:** Their inbox (they gave us their email for exactly this). Everything else, like social and ads, is out of scope for this plan.

**[CONFIRM] Do we know which group each person is in?** If the waitlist form didn't ask, the first email can ask people to choose with a single click ("I'm new to tea / I'm buying a gift / I drink tea every day"). Anyone who doesn't click gets the general version of each email.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:**
> The Steep Club is open. Each month we send you small-batch leaves we picked and tasted, so all you have to do is put the kettle on.

**Supporting messages, by group:**

| Group | Message | Proof we can use |
|---|---|---|
| Beginners | "You don't need to know anything about tea. Each box comes with a simple brewing card, and we're one reply away." | What's in the box and the brewing card **[CONFIRM]**. Mira's own story of how she started. |
| Gift buyers | "A gift that arrives again every month, a small reminder to slow down." | Gift options and lengths **[CONFIRM]**. Real last shipping date for holiday delivery **[CONFIRM]**. |
| Daily drinkers | "Leaves you wouldn't find on a supermarket shelf. We pick them in small batches and taste every one." | This month's teas and where they come from **[CONFIRM]**. |
| Everyone | "Founding members are the first people to join, and we're glad you're one of them." | Any founding-member perk only if it is real **[CONFIRM]**. |

**Message order in every email:**
1. Why care: a moment of calm in a busy month.
2. What it is: a monthly box of leaves we picked.
3. Why us: small batch, tasted by us, a real person to reply to.
4. What to do: one clear button ("Join The Steep Club" or "Give The Steep Club").

**Brand guard-rails for this campaign** (from `brand/voice.md`):
- No health language. Not even "relaxing," "calming for your body" or "good for you." Talk about the *moment* (a pause, a warm cup), not about effects on the body.
- No fake urgency. A holiday shipping date is allowed **only if it is the real, dated cut-off**. No "last chance," no countdowns, no "only X spots."
- No "best tea," "number one" or "unbeatable."
- No made-up reviews. Use only things real customers or waitlist members actually said **[CONFIRM: do we have any?]**.
- "Founding member" is fine because it is literally true. Don't call the club "exclusive."
- Sign off every email: *Happy steeping, Mira at Kettlebird Tea*.

## 4. Channel strategy

**Email (the only channel)**
- **Why it fits:** The whole audience is a list of people who asked to hear from us. Email is cheap, personal and easy to measure.
- **Format:** Short, plain-looking emails that read like a note from Mira. One main photo at most, and one button. Tone and offer change by group, but the core message stays the same.
- **Effort:** Low to medium. About 7 launch emails, with 2 to 3 versions of some, plus a short welcome series for new members.
- **Budget:** Most of our small budget goes here: the email tool plus time to write and test.

**Owned support pieces (required, not extra channels):**
- **Steep Club sign-up page** on our website, with a clear gift option. Every email button links here. **It must be live and tested before 7 December.**
- **Welcome emails** for new members, so people who join feel looked after straight away and get no more launch emails.

**Not in this plan:** paid ads, influencers, PR. If we fall short by Week 3, see Section 9 for low-cost options before thinking about paid.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | What it looks like | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| **15% is too high for this list** | Week 1 ends below ~90 members | Check the open and click rates per group to find the weak spot. Rework E3 and E4 for that group. Send a plain-text "personal note from Mira" version to people who opened but didn't click. If a real founding perk is possible, introduce it then (never as fake urgency). |
| **Old or stale waitlist** | Low opens, bounces, spam complaints | Before 7 Dec, remove addresses that bounce and check when each person joined. Send E1 to the most recent sign-ups first, then the rest a few hours later. |
| **Holiday inbox noise** | Lower opens 21 Dec – 1 Jan | Light sends, no Christmas Eve to 27 Dec emails, and a gentle "slow down" tone that stands out from loud sales. |
| **Gift timing confusion** | Gift buyers miss delivery or get angry | Use only the real, dated shipping cut-off. Offer a digital or printable gift card so gifts can still be given on the day. |
| **Slipping into banned claims** | Copy says "relaxing," "detox," "last chance" | Every email goes through the brand review before sending. The guard-rails in Section 3 are the checklist. |
| **Sending to real people by mistake while testing** | Test hits real inboxes | Tests use only the made-up practice list in `leads/`. The real send is a separate, deliberate step. |
