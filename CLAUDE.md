# Email Studio

This folder is an email marketing studio. We use it to plan and write email campaigns for a small business.

## Folders

- `brand/` — our brand voice and style rules.
- `leads/` — practice lists of people (made up, never real).
- `campaigns/` — one folder per campaign: the plan and its emails.
- `notes/` — research, decisions and working notes.

## Rules

- Write in plain English.
- Never send email to real people.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
