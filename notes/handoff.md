# Handoff: subject angle for email 01 "Doors are open"

- **Tea-curious beginners:** an easy, friendly start with no "tea snob" feel and no fear of brewing it wrong.
- **Gift buyers:** an easy, thoughtful gift that arrives on time, with clear details on what the person gets and how long it lasts.
- **Daily drinkers:** variety and quality, meaning leaves they wouldn't find on their own.
- **Angle A (a calm daily ritual):** for example "A quiet moment for your daily cup". It talks about the moment, not the body, but it doesn't say the club is open.
- **Angle B (discovering new teas):** for example "Discover a new leaf every month". It speaks most directly to daily drinkers.
- **Pick: Angle A for email 01.** Email 01 goes to everyone, and a calm moment works for all three groups (gift buyers too: "a reminder to slow down"). It also matches step 1 of the plan's message order.
- Pair it with the preview "The Steep Club is open, and you're first to know." so the launch still comes across. Save Angle B for E3 "Meet this month's leaves".
