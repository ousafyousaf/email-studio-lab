# Angle A: a calm daily ritual

Subject line ideas for email 01, "Doors are open". Each one is 50 characters or fewer.

1. A quiet moment for your daily cup (33)
2. Your kettle, your cup, a moment to slow down (44)
3. Make room for a slow cup each day (33)

Notes:
- These talk about the moment (a pause, a warm cup), not about any effect on the body, so there are no health claims.
- There's no urgency and no superlatives.
- None of them says the club is open. If the subject should announce the launch, pair it with a preview line such as "The Steep Club is open, and you're first to know."
