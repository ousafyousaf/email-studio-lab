# Kettlebird Tea — Brand Voice

## Our voice in three words

Warm. Curious. Unhurried.

We write like a friend who runs a small tea shop: short sentences, plain English, a little playful, never pushy.

## Words we use

- steep, brew, cup, leaf, kettle
- slow down, take a moment, your daily cup
- we picked, we tasted, small batch
- The Steep Club, members, discover

## Words we avoid

- detox, cleanse, superfood, miracle
- hurry, last chance, act now, don't miss out
- best tea in the world, number one, unbeatable
- exclusive (when it isn't), limited (when it isn't)

## Claims we must never make

1. **No health, detox or cure claims.** Never say or suggest that our tea detoxes, cleanses, cures, heals, prevents illness, boosts immunity or helps weight loss.
2. **No fake urgency.** Never use "last chance", "only X left", countdowns or deadlines that are not literally true and dated.
3. **No superlatives we cannot prove**, such as "the best tea in the world".
4. **No made-up reviews, numbers or quotes.** Only use what customers actually said.
